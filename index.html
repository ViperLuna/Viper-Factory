How to Play

Goal: run a plastic factory. Buy machine blueprints, staff them, keep them fed with plastic, and haul finished pallets to the warehouse to sell them for cash.

The cheapest blueprint is the Extruder, which makes straws — that's your first buy. Every purchase is one-time (no wages), but buying another of the same blueprint or hiring another operator costs more than the last one did.

  1. Open the Shop and buy the Extruder blueprint. It lands in your hotbar at the bottom.
  2. Press the matching number key (or click the slot) to select it, then click an empty floor pad to place it.
  3. Click the placed machine and hire an operator to staff it — unstaffed machines never run, no matter how much plastic they have.
  4. Buy plastic in the Shop. Your utility worker(s) automatically carry it from the depot to any machine running low.
  5. Once a machine finishes a pallet, your pallet jack worker(s) automatically haul it to the warehouse and sell it.
  6. Click a running machine to give it a small production boost.
  7. Spend cash in Upgrades & Staff to hire more workers and speed everyone up.

Your progress autosaves in this browser.
