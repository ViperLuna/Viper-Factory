How to Play

Goal: run a plastic factory. Buy machines, license new machine types as you grow, load blueprints onto them so they know what to make, staff them, keep them fed with plastic, and haul finished pallets to the warehouse to sell them for cash.

Machines and their operators are one-time purchases (no wages), but buying another of the same machine or hiring another operator costs more than the last one did.

  1. Open the Shop → Machines tab and buy an Extruder. It lands in your hotbar at the bottom.
  2. Press the matching number key (or click the slot) to select it, then click an empty floor pad to place it.
  3. In Shop → Workers, hire a Utility Worker, a Warehouse Worker, and an operator for your machine — unstaffed machines never run.
  4. Buy plastic in Shop → Material. Your utility worker(s) automatically carry it from the depot to any machine running low.
  5. Buy the Straws blueprint in Shop → Blueprints, then click your Extruder to load it — a machine with no blueprint has nothing to build.
  6. Once a machine finishes a pallet, your warehouse worker(s) automatically haul it out to sell.
  7. Click a running machine to give it a small production boost.
  8. Spend cash in Upgrades to speed everyone up, carry more, and eventually upgrade your warehouse workers onto towmotors.

A guided tutorial walks you through your first machine step by step — skip it anytime from the banner. Your progress autosaves in this browser.
